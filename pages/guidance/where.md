# Where to Look

You may select any paper from any publication venue that has a link to one of the listed [topics](topics), so long as you have followed our [quality guidelines](quality). If you don't have a text in mind, here are some places you can find appropriate papers:

### Systemisations of Knowledge

A Systemisation of Knowledge (SoK) brings together knowledge on a given topic from many different sources, making these papers perfect for the reading group format. You can find a list of SoK papers published at a subset of top conferences at the <a href="https://oaklandsok.github.io/" target="_blank">Systematizing SoK website</a>. A more comprehensive list is <a href="https://www.hooklee.com/Research/SoK/SoK.html" target="_blank">Shujun Li's Bibliography</a>. This includes papers from even more top conferences but also papers from less reputable venues.

### Conference Awards

Conferences often honour their best papers with *distinguished paper*, *test-of-time* and other awards. Papers honoured with such an award are likely to be engaging and of substantial quality.

Some conferences and publishers have a master list of awards across all publication years, for instance:

- <a href="https://www.iacr.org/awards/" target="_blank">IACR (CRYPTO, EuroCrypt, etc.) Awards</a>
- <a href="https://www.usenix.org/conferences/best-papers?taxonomy_vocabulary_1_tid=&title_1=Security" target="_blank">USENIX Security Awards</a>
- <a href="https://www.usenix.org/conferences/best-papers?taxonomy_vocabulary_1_tid=&title_1=SOUPS" target="_blank">SOUPS Awards</a>

Unfortunately, for other conferences, you will need to check each individual event website separately. Some are linked below for your convenience:

- ACM CSS Awards: <a href="https://www.sigsac.org/ccs/CCS2025/awards/" target="_blank">2025</a>
- IEEE S&P Awards: <a href="https://sp2026.ieee-security.org/awards_papers.html" target="_blank">2026</a>, <a href="https://sp2025.ieee-security.org/awards.html" target="_blank">2025</a>
- NDSS Awards: <a href="https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss2026/" target="_blank">2026</a>, <a href="https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss2025/" target="_blank">2025</a>
- ACM AsiaCCS Awards: <a href="https://asiaccs2025.hust.edu.vn/awards/" target="_blank">2025</a>
- IEEE EuroS&P Awards: <a href="https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/EuroSP2026/accepted_and_awards.html" target="_blank">2026</a>, <a href="https://eurosp2025.ieee-security.org/accepted_and_awards.html" target="_blank">2025</a>
