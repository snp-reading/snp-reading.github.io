# Welcome

<p class="lead" markdown="1">
The :lucide-library-big: **Security &amp; Privacy Reading Group** at the <a href="https://www.surrey.ac.uk/surrey-centre-cyber-security" target="_blank">Surrey Centre for Cyber Security (SCCS)</a> is a venue for PhD students and postdocs to explore unfamiliar topics related to security, privacy and cryptography in a friendly setting.
</p>

<div class="indent" markdown="1">

### :lucide-users:&nbsp; Membership

Our members are mostly from the SCCS, but any PhD student or postdoc within the <a href="https://www.surrey.ac.uk/computer-science-research-centre" target="_blank">Computer Science Research Centre</a> is welcome to attend.

### :lucide-messages-square:&nbsp; Format

Each week, one of our members walks the group through a research paper, textbook chapter or other resource and leads discussion of the material.

### :lucide-clock:&nbsp; Time and Place

We meet most Wednesdays at 12:30pm in 30&nbsp;BB&nbsp;03. Please refer to our [schedule](#).
