# Literature Selection

Members of the reading group take turns acting as the _speaker_ for the week. When it is your turn to be speaker, you get to choose the research paper or other text we will be covering in that week's session.

We have prepared a number of pages to help you select an appropriate text for the reading group. Please familiarise yourself with this content before submitting your text:

- [Topics](topics.md) &mdash; _A list of research areas covered by the group_
- [Where to Look](where.md) &mdash; _Some ideas for finding appropriate papers_
- [Quality Guidelines](quality.md) &mdash; _Rules for selecting texts of an acceptable quality_
- [Submission FAQs](faqs.md) &mdash; _Miscellaneous questions not answered elsewhere_
